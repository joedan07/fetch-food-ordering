01/ Problem

Ordering food still runs on phone calls and paper.

Problem statement

Small restaurants and campus kitchens take orders over phone calls and counter queues. Orders get misheard, menus go out of date, customers have no idea where their food is, and staff track everything on paper. There is no single, reliable system that connects the customer, the kitchen and the delivery person.

01

Misheard orders

Orders are taken by voice with no written record, so wrong items and wrong addresses are common.

400 Bad Request
02

Out-of-date menus

Paper menus and screenshots on WhatsApp miss price changes and don't show what's actually available.

410 Gone
03

"Where's my food?"

Once an order is placed, the customer can't see it. The only way to check is to call again.

408 Request Timeout
04

Paper-bound kitchen

Staff tally orders and payments by hand, with no record of sales or popular dishes to learn from.

500 Internal Error
02/ Solution

Every order becomes a request you can track.

Fetch is a responsive web app with three views: a storefront for customers, a console for the kitchen, and a simple view for riders. All three read from and write to the same database, so everyone sees the same order.

Customer

Live menu

Categories, veg / non-veg filters and instant search. Items the kitchen marks unavailable disappear right away.

04/ Architecture

Three tiers, one request at a time.

A classic client–server design. The browser only ever talks to PHP, and only PHP talks to the database. Hover over a tier to see what it's responsible for.
